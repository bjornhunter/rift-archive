Statement

This is an unofficial fan archive. It is not affiliated with, endorsed by, sponsored by or licensed by Warlord Games, and it is not a substitute for the rulebook, the supplements or the army lists.

No rulebook text, points value, stat line, artwork, logo or photograph of a miniature is reproduced here. Setting material is summarised in the archive’s own words and cited. All diagrams, insignia and interface graphics on this site are original work or procedurally generated.

Konflikt ’47 and all associated names, settings and marks belong to their respective owners. If a rights holder wishes something changed or removed, the archive will comply.

On accuracy. The setting was substantially revised for the current edition, and older fan summaries still circulate describing lore that has changed. Where the archive knows a statement comes from the previous edition or from community interpretation, it is graded implied or contested rather than presented as fact.

Warlord publishes narrative campaigns rather than a canonical encyclopaedia. There is no authoritative political map of 1947, no complete order of battle, and no casualty figures. The atlas on this site is an interpretation, and it says so.

Corrections are welcome; the archive would rather be corrected than confident.
