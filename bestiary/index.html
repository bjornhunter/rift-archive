Field description

The Neu Republik’s tank programme did not stop with Rift-tech; it retrofitted it. The Panther X keeps the silhouette that Allied crews already feared and replaces the gun with a hyper-velocity rail weapon that tears through armour instead of trying to defeat it. Schreckwulfen

Field description

The soldier-enhancement division at Dresden built its serum from reptile, bat and alligator material, and the Schreckwulfen is what that looks like when it works. First committed in 1945, they broke Allied infantry formations badly enough that every other power began its own programme in response. They are not subtle, not controllable in the way a soldier is controllable, and not required to be.

Deployment note

Close-quarters shock troops. Get them into contact and keep them there; every turn spent crossing open ground is a turn they lose.

Sd.Kfz. 501/1 Nachtzehrer

Field description

The Nachtzehrer is the Totenkorps supply chain made mobile. Its forelimbs carry the serum and the means to deliver it, so a Nachtzehrer can convert a field of casualties into a platoon without returning to depot. The Infernospinne variant replaces the serum with napalm, for when the programme produces something that should not be recovered.

Deployment note

Follow behind Totenkorps and convert losses. Do not treat it as a fighting vehicle; treat it as infrastructure that can defend itself.

Stahltruppen

Field description

The Neu Republik’s answer to the problem of infantry survivability is not more men but better shells. Stahltruppen are sealed in Rift-derived plate heavy enough to shrug off rifle and light machine-gun fire, and equipped to keep advancing through it. Thor

Field description

The Thor is the Axis answer to the question of what happens when a tank is not big enough. Six legs, a hull like a bunker, and a weapons fit that ranges from hyper-velocity guns to Schwerefeld gravity projectors that crush armour without penetrating it. It is the single most expensive thing on most battlefields, and the Neu Republik builds it anyway.

Deployment note

Mobile strongpoint. Its Rift Die is the point of the machine: a surging Thor is a different weapon from an exhausted one. Protect the die.

Axis · occult · Undead infantry

Totenkorps

Field description

Not a unit so much as a supply chain. Totenkorps regiments are assembled from the casualties of the Eastern and Western fronts, injected with a serum derived from the Dresden Rift, and issued uniforms stripped from the dead of both sides. They do not rout, do not take cover, and do not stop when they should. Warlord’s own description is unflinching: even when defeated, they can shamble back to life and stumble on, limbs missing.

Deployment note

Attrition formations. Use them to absorb fire that would break a living platoon, then close and swamp. The German officer corps is divided on them; the German quartermaster is not.

Totenrotte Commander

Field description

Each Totenrotte commander is personally approved by the Harvestman, and issued the advanced body armour of the Axis shocktroopers. Their function is not tactical cleverness but direction: the dead will walk toward the enemy on their own, but they need a shepherd to walk them at the right one. The bodyguard detail exists because a commander who can raise the dead is a commander worth shooting.

Deployment note

Attach to Totenkorps formations to keep them moving and to exploit their Fearless. Treat the bodyguard as ablative armour with a rifle.
