The war that
refused to end

It is 1947. Humanity has breached the barrier between realities, and the Second World War never stopped. This is a field archive of everything the record contains — the wound at Los Alamos, the fire at Dresden, five powers, and the machines and monsters they built.

01 · The divergence

Three moments
that broke history

The war proceeds exactly as ours does until February 1944. Everything after that is the consequence of two detonations and one decision.

— Filed under: Rift, chronology

I The Trinity test 6 February 1944 · Los Alamos. The bomb works, and opens something that does not close. → II Dresden March 1944. A second Rift opens in the crater. The Green Vault steps out of the shadows. → III The crusade 1945. A President walks out of the Rift renewed, and the grand alliance dies in a broadcast. →

02 · The powers

Five answers
to the same wound

Each power exploits the Rift differently. That difference is the war.

AX-01

Axis

Neu Republik · The Green Vault

The first power to make the Rifts serve it, and the only one whose mastery arrived complete. When the atomic fire fell on Dresden, something answered from inside the wound.

NecromancyPowered armour

US-02

United States

Arsenal of Democracy · Manifest Destiny

America opened the first Rift by accident, in the New Mexico desert, and has spent every day since trying to prove the accident was providence. The Trinity test at Los Alamos tore something in the world; within a year a President who had been dying walked out of that tear renewed, and told his country that God had spoken to him..

BC-03

Commonwealth

The Empire Answers · Automation

Britain watched the Rifts open and concluded that the sun would not be setting on its watch. The Empire was put on a war footing answering directly to London, and the Commonwealth leaned into the one branch of Rift science it could make its own: thinking machines..

SB-04

Soviet Bloc

Science Cities · The Long War

The Soviet Bloc never had a Rift of its own. It had something arguably more dangerous: a state apparatus willing to reverse-engineer the future from salvage, and a population it was prepared to spend without limit..

EJ-05

Japan

The Emperor’s Laboratories · The Ghost War

Japan’s Rift programme is the most secret and the least explained. The laboratories on Iwo Jima produced weapons no other power can replicate and few can even describe: compression weapons that fold a target into itself, and infantry who step sideways out of the world and come back behind you..

04 · The engine

The Rift Die

Every Rift unit carries a die that exists in one of three states. Its state decides whether the technology behaves — and the setting’s whole argument about borrowed power sits inside that rule.

Press the die to cycle it. This is a narrative toy, not a rule: the actual mechanic lives in the game, and the archive does not reproduce it.

Active

State: Active

The die is live. The technology behaves as designed, which in this war is a rare and temporary condition.
