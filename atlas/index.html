Snapshot note

The year the Rifts open. The Axis holds Europe from the Pyrenees to the Donets, and the Dresden wound is weeks old.

Control zones are interpretive. Warlord publishes campaign narratives and army lists, not a canonical political map of 1947 Europe. This layer is drawn from the published chronology and should be read as an argument rather than a fact.

Two Rifts sit outside this frame: Los Alamos, New Mexico (the first, February 1944) and the Pacific theatre, where the second Rift War volume, Invasion Amerika, sets its narrative.

Operational register

Twelve places that decided nothing

01 Dresden Atomic fire, then a permanent Rift at the city’s centre. Capital of the Neu Republik in all but name, and the single strategic objective of the European war. March 1944 — present
02 Berlin Seat of the Neu Republik’s administration. The Green Vault governs from here, but the power that matters sits two hundred kilometres south. 1944 — present
03 Normandy Overlord went in against Totenkorps shock regiments. Three years later it is still the Allied starting line, and Festung Europa opens here. 6 June 1944 — 1947
04 Paris Liberated on roughly the historic schedule under de Gaulle — the last event in Europe that resembled the war everyone had planned for. August 1944
05 London Imperial general staff and the centre of Commonwealth automation research. The most heavily defended city in the world, and the least sure of its ally. 1945 — present
06 The Ruhr The industrial base that makes the Axis walker programme possible. Allied bomber doctrine has stopped trying to destroy it and started trying to capture it. 1944 — present
07 Vienna The hinge between the Axis heartland and the Balkans. Contested for two years; the front has moved less than forty kilometres. 1945 — 1947
08 Warsaw Ground zero of the Eastern stalemate, where Soviet mass meets Axis Rift-tech and neither side can convert a local success. 1944 — 1947
09 Rome The Italian peninsula has split in two: the German-backed Social Republic in the north, the Allied Kingdom of Italy in the south. 1944 — 1947
10 Moscow The Science Cities answer to Moscow. The Soviet Bloc has no Rift of its own and has decided that stolen science is sufficient. 1944 — present
11 Stalingrad Retaken, rebuilt, and turned into the Soviet Bloc’s proving ground for Rift-enhanced infantry. The city is now mostly laboratories. 1944 — 1947
12 Brest The Atlantic approaches and the western edge of the Axis submarine war. Where the first reports of undead patrols reached Allied intelligence. 1944 — 1947

Sources: Touche Critique, Festung Europa · Goonhammer introduction · Field Manual
