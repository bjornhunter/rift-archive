EJ-05 · Belligerent record

The Empire of Japan

The Emperor’s Laboratories · The Ghost War

StealthPhasingMeleeLight walkers

Insignia · reconstruction

Japan’s Rift programme is the most secret and the least explained. The laboratories on Iwo Jima produced weapons no other power can replicate and few can even describe: compression weapons that fold a target into itself, and infantry who step sideways out of the world and come back behind you.

Where the Axis fields horror as spectacle, Japan fields it as discipline. Its soldiers are devoted, its armour is light, and its entire method is to close the distance before the enemy understands the engagement has begun.

Japanese armies are versatile and fast — above average at everything, best in the war at cover, ambush and exploiting a mistake. They will not win a shooting match against a Thor. They will not offer one.

Relationship to the Rift

Practical mysticism. The Japanese programme treats the Rift as a place one can step into briefly and return from changed — hence “Shinobi Space”, the name its own soldiers gave to the phase realm.

Battlefield role

Subversive. Infantry assault supported by light armour; close quarters and concealment over firepower.

Signature methods

Ghost infantry phasing through solid matter, Assault Frame armoured infantry built for melee, Shibito undead squads, scorpion-pattern light walkers.

Cost

Fragile across the board. Japanese units must land the first blow; the second one is usually fatal.
