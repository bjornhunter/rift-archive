AX-01 · Belligerent record

The Axis

Neu Republik · The Green Vault

NecromancyPowered armourWalkersSupersoldiers

Insignia · reconstruction

The first power to make the Rifts serve it, and the only one whose mastery arrived complete. When the atomic fire fell on Dresden, something answered from inside the wound. The Green Vault — a conspiracy older than the Reich it had quietly steered — stepped out of the shadows, purged the Nazi leadership, and installed the Neu Republik in its place.

Axis doctrine is built around the shocking weight of a few terrible things: powered armour that laughs at small arms, walkers that carry naval guns, and infantry that was formerly a grave. The Green Vault does not field armies so much as demonstrations. Every engagement is an argument, made in flesh and steel, that resistance is a category error.

Its weakness is arithmetic. German units are expensive, and the Vault spends men the way a gambler spends inherited money — confidently, and faster than it earns.

Relationship to the Rift

Theology disguised as physics. The Vault treats the Rift as a door that was always meant to be opened, and its science as a form of scripture — hence the pagan names on its machines: Wotan, Thor, Nachtjäger, Nachtalben.

Battlefield role

Powerhouse. Heavy armour, superheavy walkers, genetically altered shock troops. Few units, each one a problem the enemy must solve.

Signature methods

Schwerefeld gravity weapons that crush flesh and plate alike; the Schienenkanone hyper-velocity gun that treats armour as paper; necromantic infantry to soak losses.

Cost

The most expensive force in the war. Every casualty is a programme, not a man. Attrition is the Axis’ one true fear.
