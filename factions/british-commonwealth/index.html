The British Commonwealth

The Empire Answers · Automation

AutomataAIHeavy armourCombined arms

Insignia · reconstruction

Britain watched the Rifts open and concluded that the sun would not be setting on its watch. The Empire was put on a war footing answering directly to London, and the Commonwealth leaned into the one branch of Rift science it could make its own: thinking machines.

Where the Axis grows monsters and the Americans grow miracles, the British build factories. Automated infantry march in ranks, tireless and unafraid; the MkIIC Automated Director walks behind them, and somewhere up the line an intelligence that should not exist decides what happens next.

Commonwealth armies are the most straightforward in the war to command and the hardest to shock. They do not have the heaviest tank or the strangest weapon; they have the correct answer, in quantity, at the right moment.

Relationship to the Rift

Engineering ethics in a war zone. The Commonwealth distrusts Rift-energy mysticism and prefers to encode it: if a machine can be taught to be brave, the Empire does not have to spend a man learning the same lesson.

Battlefield role

Balanced. Heavier armour than the US, less monstrous than the Axis, with excellent long-range fire support.

Signature methods

Automated infantry and directors, Galahad powered armour, walkers licensed from American design, Cerberus attack squads.

Cost

Nothing in the list is a trap; nothing is a miracle either. Mistakes are punished slowly rather than immediately.
