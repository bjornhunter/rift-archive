The United States

Arsenal of Democracy · Manifest Destiny

Jump infantryTeslaWalkersCrusade

Insignia · reconstruction

America opened the first Rift by accident, in the New Mexico desert, and has spent every day since trying to prove the accident was providence. The Trinity test at Los Alamos tore something in the world; within a year a President who had been dying walked out of that tear renewed, and told his country that God had spoken to him.

What followed was not a collapse into fanaticism so much as a re-founding. The war became a crusade, the alliance with the Soviet Bloc became an obstacle, and the United States became a nation that builds walkers the way it once built Liberty ships — in rows.

Doctrine is mobility and firepower: Firefly jump infantry dropping behind a line, Tesla coils crackling along a flank, and more walker patterns than any other power can field. American units hit hard, move fast, and do not like standing still to be hit back.

Relationship to the Rift

Manifest Destiny with a scientific instrument in one hand and a Bible in the other. The Rift is treated as a national inheritance, and the Los Alamos site as a shrine with a laboratory attached.

Battlefield role

Cannons. High-mobility hit-and-run, deep-strike raids, jump infantry and medium armour. Fire on the move without penalty.

Signature methods

Firefly jump troops, Tesla weaponry, the widest walker catalogue in the war, and the Paragon programme’s psychic soldiers.

Cost

Glass cannon. Superb short-range punch and speed, but units that stop moving tend to stop existing.
